Matchday Recap

Matchday Recap publishes short football match recaps for the Bundesliga, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1.

Owner console

Connect the owner's TikTok account and send a generated recap video to the TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner reviews and publishes it in the TikTok app. Nothing is published automatically.

Step 1. Connect your TikTok account (Login Kit).

Connected as

Step 2. Choose a recap video (MP4, up to 64 MB) and send it to your TikTok inbox (Content Posting API).

What it does

After a match has finished, the service takes publicly available match data (score, scorers, minutes, basic statistics, league table) and turns it into a short vertical video with graphics, subtitles and a synthetic commentary voice. The videos are posted to the TikTok account operated by the service owner.

What it does not do

  • It does not use broadcast footage or other licensed match footage.
  • It does not collect or store personal data of viewers or other TikTok users.

Videos are marked as AI-generated content where the platform provides this option.
